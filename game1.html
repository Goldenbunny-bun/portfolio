Welcome to Bunny Bake Off!

Race through the pastel hedge garden to gather 15 Carrot Cake ingredients and bring them back to your Pink Baking Table before the rival bunnies finish theirs!

🐾 Carry Limit: 3 Items

Your paws hold 3 ingredients at once. Drop them off at your bottom-left pink baking table to bank them!

🧺 Special Item: Basket (5!)

Grab the glowing Picnic Basket in the garden center to carry up to 5 ingredients at once!

🕳️ Dig Holes to Sabotage

Press Space / Dig to dig a garden hole! Rival bunnies who fall in drop their loot and restart at their table.

🍰 Guard & Steal Tables

Dodge rivals sneaking toward your table to steal—or sneak up to their tables to swipe an ingredient!
